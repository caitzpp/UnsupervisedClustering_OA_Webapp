In the **Embedding Explorer**, you can **move freely through the embedding space** to explore how the samples are positioned relative to each other.  
Each point represents an X-ray sample, and the distances between them reflect how similar or different the samples are according to the model.

Use the **dropdown menu** to choose what the **legend** represents:  
- **Clusters** – shows how the model grouped the samples  
- **KL-Score** – shows the Kellgren–Lawrence severity grades
- **Noise Points** – highlights samples that the model marked as outliers (not assigned to any cluster)

You can also **click on any point** to **view the corresponding X-ray image** and examine its characteristics more closely.
