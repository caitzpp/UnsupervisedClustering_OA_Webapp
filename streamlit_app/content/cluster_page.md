In the **Cluster Gallery**, you can take a closer look at the **X-ray images within each cluster** to explore visual patterns and differences.

Images are sorted by an **anomaly score**, which indicates how much they **deviate from healthy images**.  
- By default, they are sorted in **descending order**, so the most anomalous images appear at the top.  
- You can switch to **ascending order** if you prefer to start from the least anomalous samples.

The first images you will see belong to the cluster label -1. This means that the model marked them as outliers and they were not assigned to a specific cluster.

As you scroll through the images, look for **patterns or trends** within each cluster — for example, how the images change as the anomaly score decreases.
